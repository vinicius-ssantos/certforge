# ADR 0004: Allow only authorial certification content

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-07-30

## Context

Exam dumps and copied commercial material create legal, ethical, and educational risks. They also encourage memorization and make an open-source portfolio project difficult to trust.

## Decision

Accept only original CertForge questions, explanations, and examples, supported by authoritative public references and technical review. Prohibit leaked, reconstructed, copied, or closely paraphrased exam material.

## Consequences

- Content growth is slower and requires editorial effort.
- The repository remains publishable and defensible.
- Learning quality and reviewer accountability become first-class concerns.
- Contributions that cannot demonstrate provenance are rejected.

## Rejected alternatives

- Importing dumps with disclaimers.
- Treating external question-bank licenses as an initial product dependency.
