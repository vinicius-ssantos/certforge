# Success Metrics

## Product outcome

CertForge succeeds when learners make measurable progress in understanding, not merely when they generate activity.

## Initial product metrics

- Completed study sessions.
- Questions answered and reviewed.
- First-attempt accuracy by topic.
- Accuracy trend by topic over time.
- High-confidence incorrect answers.
- Low-confidence correct answers.
- Median response time by question type and difficulty.
- Repeated misconception frequency.
- Percentage of published questions with complete review metadata.

## Personal preparation indicators

- Consistent study frequency without requiring streak pressure.
- Declining recurrence of the same misconception.
- Balanced topic coverage.
- Improving performance on unseen or sufficiently rested questions.
- Stable performance under time constraints once mock exams are introduced.

## Anti-metrics

The project will not optimize solely for:

- time spent in the application;
- number of clicks or page views;
- streak length;
- raw question volume;
- a single readiness score without an explanation of its inputs.

## `v0.1.0` acceptance signal

The first release is valuable when one learner can complete repeated topic sessions, inspect explanations and history, and identify at least one evidence-backed weak topic without using external spreadsheets.
