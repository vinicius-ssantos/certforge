# Domain Glossary

- **Certification track:** A family of preparation content, such as professional Java certification.
- **Exam version:** A specific exam objective set and versioned preparation target.
- **Topic:** A bounded knowledge area within an exam version.
- **Question:** The logical authoring identity across revisions.
- **Question revision:** An immutable, reviewable version of a question, its options, answer, explanation, and metadata.
- **Option:** A selectable answer candidate belonging to one question revision.
- **Study session:** A bounded learner activity containing selected question revisions and attempts.
- **Attempt:** The immutable result of one submitted answer.
- **Confidence:** The learner's self-reported certainty at submission time.
- **Topic progress:** An evidence summary derived from attempts; not a manually edited grade.
- **Published:** Eligible for use in new study sessions.
- **Deprecated:** Retained for history but excluded from new sessions.
- **Mock exam:** A timed assessment assembled from an explicit blueprint.
- **Runner:** The future isolated service responsible for compiling and executing untrusted snippets.
