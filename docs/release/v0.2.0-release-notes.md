# CertForge v0.2.0, Adaptive Review: release notes

**Released 2026-10-03.** The [readiness review](v0.2.0-readiness.md) records the gates this passed, what a person checked by hand, and what that does not amount to.

## What it is

`v0.1.0` let a learner study by topic and see what they had done. This one tells them **what is worth doing next, and why**.

The queue is derived from their own attempts every time it is read. Nothing is stored, so it cannot disagree with the evidence it came from, and there is no scheduler state to drift ([ADR 0013](../adr/0013-derived-review-queue.md)).

## What you can do

**As a learner**

- Open **Review** and see what is worth revisiting, grouped by topic, ordered by what the reason means rather than by a score.
- Read **why** each question is there, in words:
  - *Wrong, and you were sure* — answered incorrectly having said you were confident.
  - *Wrong* — answered incorrectly.
  - *Right, but unsure* — answered correctly having said you were guessing.
  - *Due for recall* — answered correctly and confidently long enough ago to prove again.
- Practise exactly the questions the queue listed, a topic at a time, rather than a fresh draw.
- See on **Progress** where you have been repeatedly wrong while confident, with the attempts and the number of distinct questions counted separately.

## The one that matters

**Wrong while confident.** A wrong answer given confidently is a misconception rather than a gap, and it is more dangerous precisely because nothing has told the learner to look again. Accuracy cannot see it: it counts that answer exactly like one the learner knew was a guess.

It is the reason the product has asked for a confidence level on every answer since `v0.1.0`, and this is the release where that becomes worth something.

## How a question rests, and comes back

After a correct and confident answer a question waits a day. Each further consecutive confident-correct answer doubles the wait, up to 64 days. **Any wrong answer resets it to the start.** A correct answer given at low confidence does not rest at all — accuracy counting that as a success is the blind spot.

That is a rule, not a tuned formula, so it can be told to a learner in one sentence and argued with on evidence.

## Known limitations

- **The recall rule has not been used by anyone studying for real.** It is a stated rule precisely so it can be changed; nothing here claims the numbers are the right ones.
- **The queue is about revisiting, not discovery.** A question you have never attempted never appears in it. The page says how many those are.
- **Practising is a topic at a time**, because a study session belongs to one topic. There is no single action that practises the whole queue across topics.
- **No readiness, mastery or predicted score**, here or anywhere. The queue ranks evidence; it does not predict an exam result, and the misconception counts say so on the page.
- **The mock exam API and its persistence are present but unreachable.** They are `v0.3.0` work that landed while this was being built; there is no learner-facing surface for them in this release and nothing here claims they are finished.
- Everything `v0.1.0` listed as a limitation still applies: one backend instance, no TLS in the images, no email verification or password reset, no tracing.
- **The content pack is 150 questions of which 130 are unreviewed**, and nothing publishes them automatically. A fresh install still has no questions.

## Deferred

Mock exams and their learner experience, a queue that spans topics in one sitting, per-item scheduling state such as an ease factor (rejected by ADR 0013, which says what a later release would have to supersede to add one), and anything that predicts readiness.
